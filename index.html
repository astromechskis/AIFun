Structural Performance Metrics (Table 3)

The structural performance depends on both geometry and material parameters:

  • Fundamental Frequency (f₀): Measures the stiffness of the deployed structure
  • Packaged Dimensions (L₁, L₂, L₃): Envelope of the folded structure
  • Packaging Ratio (p): Ratio of enclosed volumes in deployed vs. folded configurations

Key material parameters include:

  • Young's modulus (E)
  • Poisson's ratio (ν)
  • Linear/areal density (ρ)
  • Material thickness (t)
  • Wire diameter (Dₘᵢₙₑ)
